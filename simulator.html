What the virtual result means

Model version: illustrative-v1. The renderer uses a rounded shape when gravity is below 0.2 g and airflow below 3; otherwise it shows an elongated or airflow-distorted shape. Brightness index = 100 × [0.25 + 0.75 × (oxygen − 10) / 25], for oxygen from 10–35%. These are drawing rules, not experimentally calibrated combustion equations. A round Moon flame is a renderer choice, not a universal lunar prediction.
