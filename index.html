Why does fire
change in space?

Take gravity out of the picture, and familiar flame behavior starts to change. Here’s the science behind the difference.

Browse NASA combustion studies ↗
01

Buoyancy shapes the familiar.

On Earth, heated gases generally rise, producing an upward-moving flow that can elongate diffusion flames.

02

Microgravity changes the balance.

In weak buoyancy, oxygen transport through diffusion becomes more important. Some flames become rounder.

03

Understanding fire makes space safer.

NASA combustion research helps scientists understand flame spread, extinction, ventilation, and fire hazards in space.
